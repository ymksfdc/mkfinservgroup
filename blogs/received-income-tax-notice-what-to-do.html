Received an Income Tax Notice? What to Do Next

A response framework to reduce risk and avoid panic decisions.

Receiving a tax notice does not always mean a major problem. Many notices are automated and can be resolved with correct data and timely response.

Step 1: Verify Authenticity

Always verify notice details on the official income tax portal. Check document identification number, section reference, and due date.

Step 2: Understand the Notice Type

  • 143(1): Intimation for mismatch after processing.
  • 139(9): Defective return notice.
  • 142(1): Inquiry for specific details/documents.
  • 148: Income escaping assessment (high priority response).

Step 3: Collect Supporting Documents

  • ITR acknowledgement and filed return copy
  • Form 16, Form 26AS, AIS/TIS
  • Bank, capital gain, and deduction proofs
  • Previous reply records (if any)

Step 4: Submit Reply Before Deadline

Draft a factual response with supporting attachments. Missing the deadline can escalate penalties and further scrutiny.

When to Consult a Professional

If the notice involves complex income, multiple years, or reassessment sections, expert review is strongly recommended before you submit a reply.
