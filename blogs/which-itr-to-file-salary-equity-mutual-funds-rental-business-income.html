Which ITR to File for Salary, Equity, Mutual Funds, Rental or Business Income (AY 2026-27)

Use this simple framework when your income is from multiple sources.

If you have salary plus investments or rental income, choosing the correct ITR form is the first major step. Wrong form selection can lead to defective return notices and delays in processing.

Quick Rule of Thumb

  • ITR-1: Resident individual with simpler income pattern and eligibility conditions.
  • ITR-2: No business/professional income, but capital gains / multiple house properties / higher complexity.
  • ITR-3: Business or professional income (including many trading/freelance situations requiring business reporting).
  • ITR-4: Presumptive business/profession for eligible resident taxpayers under sections 44AD/44ADA/44AE.
  • Salary + side business/freelancing: usually moves to ITR-3 (or ITR-4 only when presumptive conditions are fully met).

Income Type vs Likely ITR Form

Your Income Mix Generally Used Form Notes
Salary + one house property + interest income ITR-1 Only if all ITR-1 conditions are satisfied for the relevant AY.
Salary + equity/mutual fund capital gains ITR-2 (sometimes ITR-1) ITR-1 may be available only for eligible limited LTCG u/s 112A cases; otherwise use ITR-2.
Salary + rental income from more than one house property ITR-2 ITR-1 is generally not used in this scenario.
Freelance/consultancy/business income (regular books) ITR-3 Use when income is under business or profession and not presumptive-only.
Small business/profession under presumptive scheme ITR-4 Subject to resident status, income thresholds, and other conditions.

How to Decide Correctly (Do This Every Year)

  1. List all income heads: salary, house property, capital gains, business/profession, and other sources.
  2. Check if you have any exclusion triggers (for example: multiple house properties, business income, specific asset categories, or special-rate incomes).
  3. Use the official portal option: Help me decide which ITR form to file.
  4. Cross-check with AIS, Form 26AS, and broker/rental/business statements before final submission.

Important Note for AY 2026-27

Form eligibility rules can change by assessment year and utility updates. This guide is educational and should be validated against the latest conditions on the official Income Tax e-filing portal at the time of filing.

Validation sources used: official Income Tax e-filing help pages for return applicability, plus comparative reference articles from ClearTax and TaxBuddy.
