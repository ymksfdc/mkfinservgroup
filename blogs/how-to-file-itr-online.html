How to File ITR Online in India (FY 2025-26)

A clear checklist and filing process for individuals and professionals.

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) on time helps you avoid penalties and keeps your financial profile strong for loans and visas. This guide explains the process in plain language.

Who Should File ITR?

  • Individuals with income above the basic exemption limit.
  • Taxpayers who want to claim TDS refunds.
  • People with foreign assets or foreign income.
  • People with high-value financial transactions.

Documents Required Before You Start

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 (if salaried)
  • Interest certificates and bank statements
  • Capital gain statements (if applicable)
  • Deductions proof (80C, 80D, home loan interest, etc.)
  • AIS and Form 26AS cross-check

Step-by-Step ITR Filing Process

  1. Log in to the income tax e-filing portal.
  2. Select assessment year and correct ITR form.
  3. Confirm pre-filled personal and income details.
  4. Add deductions and exemptions correctly.
  5. Validate tax payable/refund and submit return.
  6. Complete e-verification (Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or EVC).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Choosing the wrong ITR form.
  • Not reporting freelance, FD, or other income.
  • Skipping AIS/Form 26AS reconciliation.
  • Forgetting final e-verification.

Important Timeline

For most individual taxpayers, the normal due date is generally 31 July following the financial year, unless officially extended by the department.
