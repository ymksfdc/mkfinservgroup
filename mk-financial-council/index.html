Terms & Legal Framework

Under the Digital Services and Consumer Protection Guidelines (India)

1. Recognition Nature

Mk Financial Council is a voluntary recognition program by Mk FinServ Group™. It does not confer any legal or statutory status and does not replace or replicate any government certification or regulatory approval. This initiative operates within the framework of applicable Indian laws, including the Income Tax Act, 1961 and the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, and is independent of any government department. In accordance with the Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950, we explicitly state we are not a government agency.

2. Data Privacy (DPDP Act)

Member data provided for verification (PAN, Aadhaar, ITR details) is processed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Data is stored locally in Indian servers and used only for internal verification purposes.

3. Usage of Insignia

The Council Badge remains the intellectual property of Mk FinServ. Members are granted a non-exclusive license to display the badge for marketing as long as their compliance status is active. Misuse leads to immediate revocation.

4. Disclaimer of Liability

Membership does not guarantee success in loan approvals, visa applications, or tenders. It is a record of past compliance. Mk FinServ is not liable for any third-party financial decisions based on a member's council status.

Mandatory Statutory Declaration:

"The Mk Financial Council is a private professional recognition initiative. It is not an 'Institutional Body' under the Companies Act nor a 'Regulatory Authority' under SEBI/RBI/IRDAI. Membership signifies that the individual/entity has engaged our professional services for tax/financial discipline and has passed our internal benchmarks for consistency."
