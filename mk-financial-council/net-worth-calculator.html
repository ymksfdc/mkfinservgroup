ℹ️ About Net Worth Calculation

What is Net Worth?

Net worth is the total value of all your assets minus your liabilities. It represents your true financial standing at any point in time.

Assets & Liabilities

Assets include real estate, investments, cash, vehicles and gold. Liabilities cover loans, credit card dues, tax payables and other obligations.

Why Track Net Worth?

Regular tracking helps you monitor progress, set meaningful goals, plan for retirement, and make confident, informed financial decisions.
