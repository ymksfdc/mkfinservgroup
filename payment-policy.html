Payment Policy

Mk FinServ Group • Trust & Transparency

This Payment Policy governs all financial transactions between Mk FinServ Group and its clients. By availing our services and/or making payment, the client agrees to comply with the terms outlined below.

1. Accepted Modes of Payment

  • UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
  • Bank Transfer (NEFT / IMPS / RTGS)
  • Direct Bank Deposit
  • Authorized Digital Payment Gateways

Official payment details shall be communicated only through authorized invoices or verified communication channels of Mk FinServ. Please verify bank details before initiating large transfers.

2. Advance Payment Requirement

To maintain service excellence, certain assignments require an advance payment (full or partial) prior to commencement. Mk FinServ reserves the right to withhold initiation or filing of services until payment confirmation is successfully reconciled.

3. Invoicing and Due Dates

  • Digital invoices will be sent to the registered email/WhatsApp.
  • Payment is due within the timeframe specified on the invoice.
  • Delayed payments may attract a grace period of 3 business days, after which services may be paused.

4. Statutory & Government Fees

Fees paid towards Government portals, statutory filings, stamp duties, or third-party expenses are disbursed immediately and are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.

5. Refund & Cancellation

Professional fees represent the time and expertise allocated to your file. Once work has been initiated, fees are generally non-refundable.

In exceptional cases, refund requests will be reviewed by our compliance team on a case-by-case basis.

6. Confidentiality

We employ industry-standard encryption and protocols to ensure your payment data and transaction history remain confidential. We do not store sensitive credit/debit card information on our servers.

By proceeding with a transaction, the client acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to the financial terms of Mk FinServ Group.
