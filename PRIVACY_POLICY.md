## 4. Data Security

### Protective Measures:

- ✅ **HTTPS/TLS Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted
- ✅ **Subresource Integrity (SRI)**: External scripts verified with cryptographic hashes
- ✅ **Content Security Policy**: Restricts injection attacks
- ✅ **No Sensitive Data Storage**: Financial calculations stay on your device
- ✅ **Regular Security Audits**: Code reviewed for vulnerabilities

### Your Responsibility:

- Keep your browser and device updated
- Use strong, unique passwords for any accounts
- Clear browser cache if using shared devices
- Do not share your unique WhatsApp chat links publicly
